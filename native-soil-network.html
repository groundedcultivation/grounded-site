Native Soil Network

Growing in the ground

An opt-in network of Australian farms growing in their native soil, sharing their stories and learning from each other.

The Native Soil Network brings together outdoor farms that grow in the ground. Each farm tells its own story: the land, the soil, the climate and the people working it. The network opens up collaboration between farms and a shared research program on regional terroir.

Joining is free

There's no cost to join, now or later. Each farm approves its own profile before anything is published, and we never publish exact locations or security details.

Express your interest

The expression-of-interest form opens soon. In the meantime, email info@groundedcultivation.com.au with your farm's region (not your address).
