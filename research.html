The Native Soil Project: 2026/27 Pilot Study

The Native Soil Project is now underway with its first-year pilot study. Two partner sites less than an hour apart, one hosted by Tom Forrest in the Yarra Valley and the other by Dr Mary Cole in Cardinia Shire, are growing the same industrial hemp clones under a shared program that tightly manages variables, so each site grows its plants as similarly as possible. The pilot asks a simple question: how much do small differences in microclimate and soil type shape the flowers a plant produces? This is the terroir effect. Through close site-to-site observation across the season, and laboratory analysis of flower chemistry at harvest, the study will record how place shows up in the crop. The pilot is also a test of the method itself: where it can be improved, what it will take to fund, and how to grow it into a larger study with more partner sites and genetics, building towards a regional map and, in time, an appellation framework. Follow the study's progress here and on the Grounded Cultivation Instagram.
