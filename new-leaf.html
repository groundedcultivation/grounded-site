Where they grow

The farm sits on the low, flat plains that run down towards Western Port. This country was once part of a vast freshwater system. Over a century ago it was drained by a network of channels and turned into farmland. What was left behind is a dark, peaty topsoil that's unusually rich in organic matter, sitting over heavy, grey, gritty clay. Benefiting from a maritime climate, summers are warm rather than hot, the sea air takes the edge off frost, and around 800 mm of rain falls each year.
